# Marketing Site Brief

Brief for the first marketing page at `connect.fifthstonedev.com`. Built and reviewed locally
first; no deployment or DNS changes without owner approval.

## Goal

Explain the Inquiry-to-Action pipeline to owners of 1–20 person home-service businesses and
the manual work it replaces, then invite a conversation with Fifth Stone Dev.

## Manual work it replaces

- Copying form or email inquiries into a spreadsheet
- Remembering to send each customer a "we got your request" reply
- Tracking which requests nobody has followed up on
- The owner not knowing which leads are still unhandled

## Page structure

1. Plain-language before/after of a typical inquiry.
2. Simple step-by-step pipeline visual: form → saved → confirmation email → owner alert →
   Google Sheet row → staff reminder if unhandled.
3. What stays the same: they keep their current tools.
4. What's included / not included (from `docs/product/SCOPE.md`).
5. Soft call to action to talk with Fifth Stone Dev.

## Constraints

- Brand: **Fifth Stone Connect, from Fifth Stone Dev**. Offer name: **Inquiry-to-Action**.
- Domain: `connect.fifthstonedev.com` (canonical URL and meta tags may use it; nothing deployed).
- Tone: warm, practical, understated. The reader may have been burned by an agency before.
- No invented statistics, testimonials, logos, certifications, or claims like
  "never miss a lead" / "fully hands-off".
- No pricing and no third-party vendor prices.
- Don't invent brand colors or fonts. Ask for real assets or use clearly temporary
  neutral styling.
- Location: `apps/marketing/`. Add a changelog entry.
