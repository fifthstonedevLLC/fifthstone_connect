A request comes in through your website. Here's what happens next.

One inquiry, two ways

The same Tuesday-evening request

Someone finds your website after dinner and asks about a repair. Here's how that tends to go now, and how it goes with Inquiry-to-Action.

How it often goes today

  1. The form sends an email to a shared inbox, where it sits with everything else.
  2. The customer hears nothing and isn't sure the form worked.
  3. The next morning, someone copies the details into a spreadsheet, if they remember.
  4. A few days later, nobody is quite sure whether anyone called them back.

With Inquiry-to-Action

  1. The request is saved as soon as it's submitted.
  2. The customer gets a short confirmation email from your business, worded the way you approved.
  3. You get an alert with the details, and the request is added to your Google Sheet.
  4. If it's still open past the time you choose, your team gets a reminder. The customer never sees that one.

How it works

Six steps, in order

Each request follows the same path. The request is saved before any email is sent, so if your email or Google has a bad moment, the request isn't lost. The step is retried, and we're alerted if it keeps failing.

  1. 01

    Form submitted

    Someone fills out the form on your website.

  2. 02

    Saved first

    The request is recorded before anything else happens.

  3. 03

    Confirmation email

    The customer gets a "we got your request" email from your business name.

  4. 04

    Alert to you

    You, or whoever you choose, get an email with the details.

  5. 05

    Google Sheet row

    A row is added to your spreadsheet. Optional, if you'd rather not.

  6. 06

    Reminder if unhandled

    Your staff gets a reminder while the request is still open past its due time.

What stays the same

You keep your tools

There's no new app to log into and nothing for your team to learn. We connect to what you already use.

  • Your website

    We provide a form for it, or connect one existing form tool that can send us its submissions securely.

  • Your inbox

    Alerts arrive as ordinary email, to the addresses you pick.

  • Your Google Sheet

    Requests go into a spreadsheet you own, with columns agreed up front.

  • How you call people back

    Phone, text, email: that part stays yours. The reminder just makes it harder to lose track.

What's included

What you get, and what you don't

We'd rather be clear about the edges now than surprise you later.

Included

  • One way in: a spam-protected form we provide, or one supported form tool
  • A saved record of each request that comes through it
  • A confirmation email to the customer, with wording you approve
  • An alert email to you or your staff for each new request
  • One Google Sheet, kept in step without duplicate rows (optional)
  • A staff reminder while a request is still open
  • Setup, monitoring, and retrying anything that fails, done by Fifth Stone Dev
  • Your data exported or deleted when you ask

Not included

  • A dashboard or customer portal
  • Text messages
  • Syncing with a CRM or field-service software
  • AI features
  • Marketing or follow-up campaigns to your customers
  • Payments or invoicing
  • Medical, card, or other regulated data
  • 24/7 support: help is during business hours
  • Compliance certifications
